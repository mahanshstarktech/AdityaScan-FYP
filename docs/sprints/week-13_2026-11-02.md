**Technical Description & Deliverables:**  
Construct `ActiveRegionGraphBuilder` in `backend/pipeline/ml/active_region_graph_builder.py`. Physics grounding: Coronal loops interconnect bipolar active regions across the solar photosphere, with connectivity decaying exponentially as $w_{ij} = \exp(-\Delta\theta / \theta_0) \cdot (1 + \kappa \cdot P_{ij})$, where $\theta_0 \approx 30^\circ$ is the characteristic coronal arcade scale and $P_{ij}$ models magnetic polarity coupling.
